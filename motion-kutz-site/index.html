Our Story

Built Different,
Cut Sharp

Motion Kutz is a solo-barber studio tucked into Denver's Highlands neighborhood — a space designed to feel like nothing else in the city. Step inside and you're greeted by hexagonal LED ceiling lights and floor-to-ceiling manga and comic collage walls. The aesthetic isn't an accident; it's the whole vibe.

ChubzBlendzz brings serious craft to every chair: clean fades, precision lineups, and freestyle designs that hit different. Whether you're a first-timer or a regular, you leave looking and feeling your best.

Rated 4.8 stars on Google with 21+ reviews from real Denver heads — the reputation speaks for itself. Come see why Motion Kutz is quickly becoming the go-to shop in the Highlands.
